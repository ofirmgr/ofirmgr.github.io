Privacy Policy – Conversation Assistant Contacts

Effective September 15, 2026

Conversation Assistant Contacts is a personal tool operated by Ofir Meguri for use with a single Google account (ofirmgr@gmail.com). It is not offered to the public, and no other users are invited to sign in.

Data accessed

With the read-only Google Contacts permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts.readonly), the tool reads the names and phone numbers of the account's own contacts. It does not read any other contact fields, and it cannot create, change, or delete contacts.

How the data is used

Contact names and phone numbers are used only to match phone numbers on the operator's own call recordings to contact names, so that the resulting transcripts are labeled with the other participant's name.

Storage

Contact names and phone numbers are cached on the operator's own computer in a file readable only by the operator's user account, and the cache is refreshed at most once a day. The Google authorization token is stored in the macOS Keychain on the same computer. Contact names applied to transcripts are saved with those transcripts in the operator's own Google Drive.

Sharing

Google Contacts data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with or transferred to third parties. It is not sent to the speech-to-text service used to transcribe recordings. Transcripts that include contact names may be read by AI assistant tools that the operator runs on the operator's own computer, only when the operator asks those tools about the transcripts.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all further reads. The local contacts cache can be deleted from the operator's computer at any time.
